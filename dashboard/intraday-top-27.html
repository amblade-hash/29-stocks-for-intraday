How each stock is checked
  1. ST Momentum signal — the latest fresh BUY or SELL from ST Momentum Signals today (Supertrend direction + ADX > 20 + RSI > 55 / < 45 + Stoch RSI %K > 60 / < 40), on closed 5-minute candles.
  2. PI LVLS bounce — within the bounce window ending on the signal candle, a candle tagged a PI zone (R2, R1, S1, S2) and closed back outside it: low into the zone and close above it for a BUY, high into the zone and close below it for a SELL.
  3. Trend match — on the signal candle, Supertrend points the signal's way and all three Dual MACD Pro parts agree with it: fast MACD (8/13) above its signal line (9), slow histogram green and EMA 9 above EMA 21 for a BUY; the opposite for a SELL.
  4. Pause after a signal — a stock that gives a signal after 09:45 is checked (and alerted if confirmed) at the scan that sees it, then paused: until 10:45 if the signal was seen by the 10:00 scan, until 11:40 if seen after 10:00. While paused it can't send another alert; signals during a pause are ignored, and after a pause ends the next signal starts a new one.
  5. Opening rule — a BUY or SELL that fired by the 09:45 scan (opening candles) is never alerted. Its stock is on hold until the 10:15 scan; after that only a new signal that fired after 09:45 can be confirmed.

A stock is Confirmed when all three pass and Watch when a signal has two of three. Scans run at every 5-minute candle close from 09:45 to 11:45 IST while this page is open, then stop for the day. Outside that window the page shows the last session's final 11:45 result.

The final 11:45 scan is the day's report: after it, Download PDF report saves it as a PDF.

With Telegram alerts on, each new Confirmed buy or sell is posted to the Telegram channel at the scan that finds it. Nothing else is posted. Nothing is posted outside 09:45–11:47 IST, and each setup is posted once per browser, so keep the page open on one device.
